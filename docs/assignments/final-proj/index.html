Final Project

Final Project

The goal of the final project is to design an object based on a Teensy. It can take any form you wish: a sound or visual installation, a musical instrument (physical interface + synthesizer), a MIDI controller, etc. Your system must be placed outside the breadboard (e.g., inside a cardboard box, mounted on a mug, etc.). Feel free to make something with a "DIY" look (with tape, etc.).

Timeline

Milestone 1: Presenting Final Project Ideas

  • This milestone is due on November 21, 2026.
  • Prepare three final project ideas that you will present during class.
  • Make sure that at least one of these three ideas is "crazy" and that one is technically feasible.
  • Make a diagram/drawing of each idea on an A4 sheet, take photos of them, and upload these photos to this folder: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1rvl-qiQojr3slZ-nwKeEZJH1mGCrWVH1?usp=sharing naming them LASTNAME_FIRSTNAME_0.jpg where “0” is the idea number.

Final Project

  • Submission deadline: January 3, 2027.
  • Items to submit:
    • A link to a publicly accessible video presenting the project (YouTube, etc.). The format is open but it must not exceed 1:30 minutes. The video must be in English (subtitles are allowed; you do not necessarily need to speak). Particular attention should be paid to the editing/sound quality. The content of the video is completely open: a demo, a technical description, a performance, etc.
    • A one-page PDF describing the system and its features.
    • Your submission must take the form of a zip file named LASTNAME_FIRSTNAME.zip and placed on the following drive: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1vea-nJhhHaxZm_qLYJUXmFFU21TTdcEn?usp=sharing
