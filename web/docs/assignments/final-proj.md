# Final Project Prep

* Prepare three final project ideas that you will present during the next class.
* Make sure that at least one of these three ideas is "crazy" and that one is technically feasible.
* Make a diagram/drawing of each idea on an A4 sheet, take photos of them, and upload these photos to this folder: <https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1yKlkEDJ6Gd5zs5zryvohu0L429aqouzz?usp=sharing> naming them “LASTNAME_FIRSTNAME_0.jpg” where “0” is the idea number.
