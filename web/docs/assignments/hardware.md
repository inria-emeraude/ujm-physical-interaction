# Hardware Control Assignment

* **This assignment must be sent to Romain by e-mail before ??.**
* The goal of this assignment is to set up an Arduino/software pair using the software of your choice (e.g., Processing, Max/MSP, Faust, Unity, Unreal, etc.) and by creating the interface you want on the breadboard. The software may produce sound, visuals, or both.
* Your controller may be based on potentiometers, buttons, or any other type of sensor.
* Try to create something that "makes sense" and that shows good coherence between the software and the hardware. Feel free to decorate your breadboard, put it in a box, etc.
* Make a short demo video (phone recordings are fine).
* Upload this video online (e.g., YouTube).
* Send me the link to the video: romain_point_michon_at_inria_point_fr.
* Have fun!
