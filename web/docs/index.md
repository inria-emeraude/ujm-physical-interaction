# Physical Interaction Design ("Gesture Capture")

The *Physical Interaction Design* course is offered as part of the Digicrea and CCNT master's programs at Université Jean Monnet in Saint-Étienne (France). Its goal is to provide an overview of embedded electronics in the context of the arts. A wide variety of topics are covered: basic electronics, sensor technologies, microcontroller programming, embedded audio, and digital fabrication. By the end of the course, students will be able to design their own control interfaces as well as interactive installations.

More specifically, the following topics are covered:

* Digital luthery and DMIs (Digital Musical Instruments);
* Microcontroller programming (e.g., Arduino);
* Basic electronics;
* Digital fabrication fundamentals;
* Use of sensors;
* Controller creation;
* Basics of embedded audio.

A project in which students will create their own physical controller will serve as the course assessment.
